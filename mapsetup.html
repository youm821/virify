Live map setup

Admins only. Upload a picture of the ER:LC map, then match two places on it to where players really are in-game.

1. Upload the map picture

Use a square PNG, JPG, or WebP of the full map (up to 8 MB), and one you’re allowed to use.

2. Match two spots

Stand at a landmark in-game, pick yourself below, then click the same spot on the picture. Do it again at a landmark far away (ideally opposite corners).
