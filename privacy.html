Privacy

What this site keeps about you, and who can see it.

What we keep

  • Your Discord name, ID, and server roles, used to sign you in and decide which pages you can open.
  • Your linked Roblox username and ID, if you choose to link your account.
  • Your time in the server (minutes per day and team), counted while you are online.
  • Applications you submit, event sign-ups, and your site settings.

What we don’t keep

  • Your Discord or Roblox password. Sign-in happens on Discord’s own page.
  • Direct messages, or anything outside the servers and pages connected to this community.

Who can see it

  • Members can see their own profile and applications only.
  • Staff can see live server data, staff activity, and application details needed to review them.
  • Admins manage departments, rules, and settings. Staff actions are recorded in an audit log.

For staff

  • Only use player information to do your job. Don’t share it outside the staff team.
  • Don’t post logs, applications, or private notes publicly or in screenshots.
  • Report any leak or mistake to an admin straight away.

Your choices

You can unlink your Roblox account any time from the Link Roblox page. To have your other data removed, ask an admin in Discord.
